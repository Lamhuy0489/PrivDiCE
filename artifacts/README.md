# Extracted artifact directories

The ECG and Heart+ subdirectories contain the fully extracted outputs for the accepted plaintext, Official DiCE, and HE paper runs. Their aggregate manuscript tables are also copied selectively under `results/` for direct inspection.

MIMIC-IV complete artifacts are intentionally excluded because they may contain derived row-level data, counterfactual values, or model artifacts governed by the MIMIC-IV credentialed-data agreement. Only non-row-level aggregate results are public in `results/mimic/`.

Notebook source snapshots do not generally contain rendered outputs; these extracted directories and acceptance records are therefore the canonical output evidence for the redistributable datasets.
